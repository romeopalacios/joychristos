Meet Joy

About

Singer, writer, and actress from Harrisburg, Pennsylvania.

Known for her soothing yet powerful vocals, Joy has performed nationally and internationally from an early age. She was a contestant on NBC’s The Voice (Season 13) and received the Hershey Theatre Apollo Award for Outstanding Lead Actress for her performance as Dorothy in The Wiz. She is also a graduate of 1500 Sound Academy in Inglewood, California.

Blending R&B, soul, jazz, pop, gospel, and classical influences, Joy creates music that inspires authenticity, healing, and transformation. Joy’s mission is to remind people of their power within & enjoy life!
